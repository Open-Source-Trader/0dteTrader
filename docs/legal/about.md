# About 0dteTrader

0dteTrader is an open-source interface for monitoring markets and submitting options orders through brokerage accounts selected and controlled by the user.

The project is provided independently and is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, exchange, or custodian. 0dteTrader does not hold customer funds or securities and does not provide personalized investment advice.

Source code, contribution guidance, security reporting, and license information are available in the project repository.
