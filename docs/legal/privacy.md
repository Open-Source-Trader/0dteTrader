# Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-08-05

0dteTrader processes the account email, encrypted broker credentials, brokerage connection metadata, order and execution records, device push tokens, notification preferences, legal acceptances, and operational logs needed to provide the service.

Broker and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest. Credentials are used to make requests selected by the user and are not sold. Data may be sent to the user's configured broker, market-data provider, Apple Push Notification service, or Discord webhook solely to perform requested features.

Self-hosted operators control their deployment, database, retention, backups, and access. Hosted operators should publish contact and jurisdiction details alongside this policy. Users can delete their account in Profile; database cascade rules remove user-owned credentials, sessions, orders, event records, device tokens, notification settings, and acceptance records.

Security reports should follow the repository's SECURITY.md instructions. Privacy questions should be directed to the operator of the deployment being used.
