# Options Trading Risk Disclosure

Version: 2026-08-05

Options involve substantial risk and are not appropriate for every investor. Short-dated and same-day-expiration options can lose all value rapidly, exhibit extreme price changes, have wide spreads, and become difficult or impossible to close.

Market, limit, midpoint, stop, chart-triggered, and bracket behavior is not guaranteed. Quotes may be delayed or stale; triggers can gap through their levels; partial fills and broker outages can leave positions unprotected. A visual line in the app is not an exchange-held order until the broker accepts the resulting order.

Review the standardized options disclosure document supplied by your broker, understand assignment and exercise, and confirm buying power, position size, liquidity, and maximum loss before trading. Practice mode does not reproduce every live-market condition.

0dteTrader provides tools, not investment recommendations. You accept responsibility for all orders submitted through your account and for independently monitoring them at the broker.
